Training and Recovery

What it costs to learn the update rule, and what it buys when the circuit is hit. A 4-bit adder is built from 264 differentiable LUTs; a local message-passing policy is meta-trained once, offline. At deployment it runs forward passes only — so when radiation scrambles LUT configuration bits, neighbouring gates negotiate a configuration that computes the adder again. Everything here is the shipped policy: minimal-edit repair, anchored to the stored configuration, with the unanchored one kept as the ablation.
