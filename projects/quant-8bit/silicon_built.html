Self-repair costs silicon

Synthesis folds away whatever it can prove is redundant, and the backprop-trained circuit is very foldable — its entire output layer disappears into the layer before it. The self-repaired circuit is not: message passing spreads the function across gates that no longer collapse. The redundancy that makes the circuit repairable is the same redundancy that makes it big.
