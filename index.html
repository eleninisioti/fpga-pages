Self-organising digital circuits — JAX to FPGA

A circuit is a layered DAG of differentiable LUTs, configured by a local message-passing rule meta-learned once offline. At deployment it runs forward passes only: when a cosmic ray scrambles LUT config bits, neighbouring gates negotiate a configuration that computes the target function again. Everything on the FPGA itself, including the update rule.
