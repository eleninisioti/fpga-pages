Self-organising digital circuits — JAX to FPGA

A circuit is a layered DAG of differentiable LUTs, configured by a local message-passing rule meta-learned once offline. At deployment it runs forward passes only: when a cosmic ray scrambles LUT config bits, neighbouring gates negotiate a configuration that computes the target function again. Everything on the FPGA itself, including the update rule.

Operation without errors

  • One page per circuitThe rule assembling adder2, adder4, gf4, mul3 and mul4 from random soft wires — no damage, no golden to restore: the message-passing episode over the real wiring, per-node messages, memory and error, the LUT contents, and where the error gate closes.

Operation under errors

  • adder2 under errorsAll three fault models on one page. Soft errors: one scrambled LUT step by step, recovery against TMR / scrubbing / ECC / genetic repair, every method under a storm. Hard errors: cells frozen at coin flips that reject every write — only the searching methods survive, and the wall past which the task no longer fits the free bits. Parametric drift: dose shifts every gate's analog margin, no bit is ever wrong, scrubbing and ECC are structurally blind — pre-registered, predictions scored against the run.
