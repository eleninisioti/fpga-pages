Scrubbing's soft-error failure mode is rate, not repair: every fault is live until the walker's next pass, so availability = fraction of time the output is exact. Single-gate scrambles arrive as a Poisson stream; the scrubber restores golden once per period; TMR+scrub (dashed, area-scaled 3λ) masks single hits and fails when a voting domain takes two in one period. The λ axis folds storm and scrub speed into one number: quiescent LEO at a 1 Hz scrub sits far off the left edge (CFESat: ~0.27 SEU/device-day); a CREME96-class storm (10²–10⁵× quiet), a power-constrained scrub period, and a real device's megabits of config cross-section move a system onto the chart. ECC fails earlier on the same axis: one error per codeword per pass is a stricter budget than one restore per period.

The real-time panel is the fair fight: the always-on policy (repairing at its measured step time, no golden copy) against the deployed 1 Hz scrubber, both under the same upsets-per-second stream. Read the policy's line against its dashed no-storm ceiling: an anchored policy drifts off the healthy circuit past its training horizon (the edit-penalty trade), so the low-rate plateau is persistence, not storm damage — the storm-attributable knee is where the line departs the ceiling. The scrubber can buy rate margin with readback bandwidth (a 10 Hz scrub shifts its curves a decade right); the policy cannot be cranked — its margin is fixed by inference speed, and it pays nothing while healthy.

4 · Provenance & method

  • Learned cells: deployed representations only — the NCA as the integer datapath (soc.fixed), the logic CA as the hard Boolean function. No float proxies anywhere in the numbers.
  • Latency in cycles at the shipped 34-lane build's measured constants for the NCA; the logic-CA step is a stated proxy (node-multiplexed combinational rule: nodes × rule depth).
  • Energy proxy: arithmetic events per repair (MACs / gate-evals) + configuration bits written.
  • Baselines from the banked soc.baselines sweeps (same substrate, same fault model, 16-32 trials).
  • Cells present when built: adder2/blogic/s0, adder2/blogic/s1, adder2/nca/s0, adder2/nca/s1, adder4/blogic/s0, adder4/blogic/s1, adder4/nca/s0, adder4/nca/s1, mul4/blogic/s0, mul4/nca/s0. Rebuild with uv run python -m soc.study report.
